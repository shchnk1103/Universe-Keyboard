Upstream source used: Moby Word Lists by Grady Ward, release 1.0 (1996),
files 74550com.mon (common-word list, frequency order) and 354984si.ngl
(single words, validation anchor). Downloaded as the mwords archive
distributed with the SCOWL v1 source tree:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/en-wl/wordlist/v1/other/mwords.tar.gz
The original Project Gutenberg location is
https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3201.

Used for: the bundled English candidate wordlist english_wordlist_v1.json
(Tier B frequency-ranked words and part of the validation anchor set). The
wordlist is generated offline at build time and shipped statically with the
app; nothing is downloaded at runtime.
