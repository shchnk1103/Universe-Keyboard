Upstream source used: SCOWL v2020.12.07 (sizes 10-95), downloaded from
http://downloads.sourceforge.net/wordlist/scowl-2020.12.07.tar.gz
(official release page: http://wordlist.aspell.net/other/).

Used for: the bundled English candidate wordlist english_wordlist_v1.json
(Tier A very-common core: english-words.10/.20, american-words.10/.20,
british-words.10/.20, and the validation anchor set *-words.10 ... *-words.95
with variants). The wordlist is generated offline at build time and shipped
statically with the app; nothing is downloaded at runtime.
