## What's New（商店更新文案草案）

**中文：**

> 本次更新带来多项新功能与改进：
> - 双拼键盘上线：支持小鹤双拼、自然码等方案，可在设置中开启并配置键位提示。
> - 主 App 设置全面改版：设置项收敛为通用、外观、键盘、Rime 与关于四组，查找更直接。
> - 键盘快捷菜单：候选栏新增快捷设置入口，常用设置在键盘端一步直达。
> - 按键气泡：支持按键气泡反馈，九宫格字母键（2–9）同样适用，可在设置中开关。
> - 九宫格修复：部分选择候选后不再残留剩余拼音。
> - 触感与按键音更同步：统一了长按删字的滑动节奏，优化按键反馈的触发时机。
> - 系统兼容：最低支持版本调整为 iOS 17。

**English:**

> This update brings several new features and improvements:
> - Shuangpin (double pinyin) keyboard: supports Xiaohe, Ziranma and other schemes, with key legends configurable in Settings.
> - A redesigned Settings app: options are now organized into four groups — General, Appearance, Keyboard, and Rime & About.
> - Quick menu: a new quick-settings entry on the candidate bar for one-tap access on the keyboard.
> - Key pop-up callouts: press feedback bubbles now available, covering all nine-key letter-group keys (2–9), with a settings toggle.
> - Nine-key fix: no more residual pinyin left after partially selecting a candidate.
> - Tighter haptic/sound sync: smoothed the long-press delete swipe rhythm and refined when key feedback fires.
> - Compatibility: the minimum supported version is now iOS 17.
